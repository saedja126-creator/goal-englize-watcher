A stop-loss only helps if the price falls through your stop gradually. A rug can take the token to near zero in one step, so the protection is a small position. Three rugs at 40 percent leaves 21.6 percent of the account, a loss of 78 percent.

What the £100 pays for, in one sentence

About £50 a month, for two months, buys access to a service that labels wallets as skilled or not. We test whether that paid list finds better wallets to follow than the free one.

Why buy it

Free sources, such as GMGN’s wallet rankings, come first. The paid list is worth keeping only if it finds better wallets than the free one.

Why two months

One month is too short for a fair comparison. Two months gives enough signals to judge.

If you say no

Nothing breaks. The plan works with £0 of data spend. You only skip testing the paid list.

Nothing is bought, scheduled or charged until month 3, and only if you say yes.
