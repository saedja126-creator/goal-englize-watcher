A stop-loss only helps if the price falls through your stop gradually. A rug can take the token to near zero in one step, so the protection is a small position. Three rugs at 40 percent leaves 21.6 percent of the account, a loss of 78 percent.

Position size

Risk per trade

Up to 2% of the account at the stop.

15% of the account, never above £30. The desk halts and waits for you.

Project stop

Live trading halts if the live slice falls 30%: £60 on £200, £150 on £500.

One position at a time

No new scan while a position is open.

No keys in the bot
