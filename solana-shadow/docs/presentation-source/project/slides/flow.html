What the shadow desk does

Scan

New Solana tokens, checked every 30 seconds

Check

Hard facts in code: authorities, holders, liquidity

Log

Every decision saved with the check that fired

Track

Price and liquidity at +30s, +5m, +15m, +1h

Report

A weekly report: do the filters work? Does a trigger beat the control?

No wallet. No orders. No keys.

It only watches, logs and measures, in the background. Paper and live trading come later, only after the gates.

The five stages of the shadow desk. Each token is scanned, checked with hard facts in code, logged with the reason, tracked afterwards and summarised in a weekly report every Sunday evening (UTC). There is no wallet and no ordering code in this phase.
