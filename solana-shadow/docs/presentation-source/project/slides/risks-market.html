Market risks and their controls

RiskHow it is controlledLevel
Rug or sudden collapsePosition kept near 10% of the account, so one rug costs about 10%. Mint, freeze and holder checks run in code.High
Oversized positionsRisk up to 2% per trade. Daily loss limit 15%, never above £30. Project stop at −30% of the live slice.High
Thin liquidity and slippagePosition capped near 1% of pool liquidity. 2% slippage cap. Costs deducted in every report.Medium
Late signal when copying walletsMeasured at the +30s checkpoint and compared with a random-entry control group.Medium

Levels are my judgement, not measured values. Control values are proposals to tune from shadow data.

Two high risks: rugs and oversized positions. Both are handled mainly by keeping each position small, because a stop-loss does not protect against a token that collapses in one step.
