Dates are plans. The gates decide, not the calendar: if a gate fails, everything after it moves or stops.

The dated version of the plan. The shadow test is counted from 27 October 2026, the first day of real data. The three-month shadow test ends with the weekly report on Sunday 24 January 2027 and the decision on 26 January. Paper trading starts the next day. The first paid-data month starts on the 15th of February, two and a half weeks into paper trading so that there is a free-data baseline first. Live money only starts if the paper gate is met, about the end of March. Every date after the shadow test is a plan, not a promise.
