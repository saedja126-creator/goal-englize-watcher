A 10-point win-rate swing flips the month

Illustrative monthly result for the same strategy at two different win rates

45% win rate

+21%

−24%

35% win rate

Assumptions: 14% stop, 30% average win, 7% position (preset A; your preset B at 10% gives +31% and −33%), 5 trades a day, 4% round-trip friction. Edge per trade after costs: +1.8% of the stake at 45%, −2.6% at 35%.

Illustrative arithmetic. Not a forecast and not a backtest.

The same rules, the same costs, and only the win rate changes. At 45 percent the month is about plus 21 percent. At 35 percent it is about minus 24 percent. That sensitivity is why the shadow test and the cost assumptions matter more than any forecast.
